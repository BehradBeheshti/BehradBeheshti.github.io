02 Research

Learning, perception & robot systems

NSF REU · Summer 2026

Learning to Coordinate Navigation and Gaze for Object Search

A collaboration across West Virginia University, UW–Madison, and the University of Cambridge.

I trained a recurrent policy that jointly controls a mobile robot’s base and pan/tilt neck for prompt-guided object search, combining behavior cloning with reinforcement learning.

My work: semantic priors, policy training, reward design, and evaluation.

Approach +
I built an open-vocabulary region–height prior from SUN RGB-D scenes and a critic-filtered LLM teacher. A CNN–GRU policy distilled a privileged planner, then improved through reinforcement learning. I evaluated how active gaze and semantic priors contribute to object search.

The NSF REU program was hosted by WVU and UW–Madison with Cambridge collaboration. I worked with Prof. Fei Dai at WVU and joined a two-week research visit to Cambridge, presenting at Prof. Ioannis Brilakis’s lab.

DiCE Lab · San Diego State University

VR Teleoperation for Mobile Manipulation

A VR interface connecting a Meta Quest operator to a Husky A200 with dual UR5e arms, a pan/tilt neck, and stereo cameras.

My work: the ROS 2–Unity bridge, operator pose streaming, and arm-command pipeline for loco-manipulation data collection.

code ↗

System details +
I built the system around ROS 2 and Unity, with TCP communication carrying operator pose and arm commands from the Meta Quest headset. The setup brings mobile manipulation, camera control, and stereo feedback into a shared operator interface.

03 Projects

Mathematics & robotics

Expository paper · Spring 2026

Generating Functions as a Counting Tool

An expository paper on a beautiful idea: encode a counting problem in a power series, then let algebra do the counting.

I work through recurrences, partitions, Catalan numbers, Dyck paths, and exponential generating functions with derivations and examples.

Read the paper ↗

Inside the paper +
  • Recurrences & products: turning sequences and choices into algebra.
  • Partitions & compositions: how restrictions change the generating function.
  • Catalan numbers & Dyck paths: decomposing paths to derive a recurrence.
  • Exponential generating functions: counting labeled structures and composing them.
