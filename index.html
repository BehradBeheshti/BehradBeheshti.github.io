I am an undergraduate in Applied Mathematics at San Diego State University, with an emphasis in computer science. I work in the DiCE Lab, where I am advised by Reza Akhavian.

I am broadly interested in robotics and machine learning, particularly how robots can learn to perceive and interact with the world. My work spans active perception, language-informed navigation, and mobile manipulation.

Alongside robotics, I enjoy mathematics and writing about it 📚. My paper on generating functions explores how algebra can help us solve counting problems.
